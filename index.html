    One standard, from the first car to the last.

    A $12,900 Honda gets exactly what a $229,900 Rolls-Royce gets. That's the whole idea.

    1. 160 points

      Inspected on one checklist.

      Brake pads measured, tyres gauged, every light, seal and switch tested, the history report pulled and read. The list doesn't get shorter when the car gets cheaper.

    2. One turntable

      Photographed in the Light Room.

      Every car is shot on the same turntable under the same light ceiling. Nothing hides in a shadow, so what you see on this page is what's waiting on the floor.

    3. No haggling

      Priced once, out loud.

      The price on the card already includes dealer fees. No add-ons appear at the desk and no one asks what you'd like your monthly payment to be.

    4. 7 days · 500 miles

      Room to change your mind.

      Take it home, take it up the canyon, park it in your own garage. If it isn't right within seven days and 500 miles, bring it back.
